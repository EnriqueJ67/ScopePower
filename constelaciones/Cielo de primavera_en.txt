#titulo:Spring sky
#descripcion:The spring sky (March to June in the northern hemisphere) is a fascinating transition between the winter and summer constellations. The sky becomes clearer and steadier, which makes it an excellent season for observing.
Galaxies abound, especially in the region of Virgo and Coma Berenices, ideal for telescopes.
#lista:Leo;Virgo;Coma Berenices;Bootes;Canes Venatici;Hercules
